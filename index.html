    Questions We Hear Often

    I missed my ITR due date. Can I still file?

    Yes, for most people. For AY 2026-27 a belated return can be filed until 31 December 2026. The late fee is up to ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if your total income is up to ₹5 lakh), plus interest on any unpaid tax. File sooner rather than later.

    Which ITR due date applies to me?

    For AY 2026-27: 31 July for salaried individuals (ITR-1 and ITR-2), 31 August for business and professional filers who do not need a tax audit (ITR-3 and ITR-4), and 31 October if your accounts must be audited. Not sure which one is yours? Message us.

    Do I need GST registration?

    It depends on what you sell, where you sell it and your turnover. Sellers on online marketplaces generally need registration irrespective of turnover; other businesses need it once turnover crosses the limit for their category. Tell us about your work and we will say clearly whether you need it.

    I received a notice. What should I do first?

    Do not ignore it. Note the reply date printed on the notice, take a clear photo or PDF and send it to us on WhatsApp. We will explain what it asks and prepare the reply.

    Can you work with clients outside Delhi?

    Yes. Most of the work runs over WhatsApp, email and phone, so we serve clients across India.
