Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common GST and tax questions.

I missed my ITR due date. Can I still file?
Yes, in many cases a belated return can still be filed based on the applicable rules and late fees. We can review your situation and guide the correct path.
Which ITR due date applies to me?
It depends on your category and income type. Salaried individuals often have earlier filing dates than businesses and professionals, and some cases have different audit-related timelines.
Do I need GST registration?
It depends on your turnover, business activity and the way you sell. Sellers in online marketplaces often need registration regardless of turnover, while other businesses need it once they cross applicable limits.
I received a GST or income tax notice. What do I do first?
Do not ignore it. Save the notice, note the date and send us the copy on WhatsApp or email. We can explain what it means and help prepare the response.
Can you work with clients outside Delhi?
Yes. We regularly work with clients across India using WhatsApp, phone and email, so distance is not a problem.
